What if the Mediterranean Sea dried up? 🌍 #shorts

Description:
Close the Strait of Gibraltar and the Mediterranean starts to vanish. In 190 years you could walk from Tunisia to Sicily. In about 1,100 years, 90% of the sea is gone.
It sounds impossible, but it happened 5.6 million years ago (the Messinian salinity crisis). Then the Atlantic broke back in with a flood 1,000 times bigger than the Amazon.
